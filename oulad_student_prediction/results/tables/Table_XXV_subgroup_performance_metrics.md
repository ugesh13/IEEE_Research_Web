### Evaluation Guidelines & Caveats:
1. **Fixed Decision Threshold**: The identical operational threshold $\theta^* = 0.45$ was applied universally across all subgroups. No subgroup threshold tuning was performed.
2. **Confidence Intervals**: Brackets represent the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles from $B = 1,000$ student-level block cluster bootstrap resamples (`RANDOM_SEED = 42`).
3. **Non-Ranking Principle**: Subgroup rows are presented in natural or alphanumeric order. No group is ranked or classified as 'best' or 'worst'.
4. **Base Rate Interplay**: Subgroups with higher At-Risk prevalence (e.g. `disability = Yes`, `studied_credits > 120`, `num_of_prev_attempts = 2+`) naturally exhibit slightly higher precision and false-positive rates due to base rate mechanics rather than differential algorithmic bias.
