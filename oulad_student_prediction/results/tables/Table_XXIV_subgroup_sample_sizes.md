### Methodological & Small-Cell Rules:
1. **Minimum Sample Size Rule**: Subgroups with $n < 100$ test observations (`No Formal quals`: $n = 52$; `Post Graduate Qualification`: $n = 46$) are designated as having insufficient sample size for primary performance estimation to prevent unstable point estimates.
2. **Clustering Context**: The total number of unique students is reported alongside observations to account for repeated student course presentations ($4,889$ observations across $4,318$ unique students).
3. **Distinction of Strata**: Demographic/socioeconomic variables (gender, age, disability, education, IMD) are explicitly distinguished from institutional/academic enrollment strata (credits, module, presentation, attempts).
