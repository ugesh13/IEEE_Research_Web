### Institutional Error Profile Interpretation:
In early-warning academic applications, the classification error trade-off is inherently asymmetric:
1. **False Negatives (Missed Students)**: Represent high-risk students who are erroneously classified as low-risk, thereby missing proactive advising interventions. Overall cohort FNR is $17.62\%$ ($455 / 2,582$), ranging between $14.12\%$ and $22.03\%$ across strata.
2. **False Positives (False Alarms)**: Represent successful students who receive an early advising alert. Overall cohort FPR is $31.25\%$ ($721 / 2,307$). In higher-risk demographic segments (e.g. `disability = Yes`, `IMD 0-10%`), FPR is modestly higher ($39.25\%$, $39.90\%$), which is mechanically consistent with their higher base rate of academic difficulty.
