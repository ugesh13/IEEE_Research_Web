## ⚠️ DOCUMENTED OPEN RISKS & REPRODUCIBILITY AUDIT FINDINGS

### Open Risk 1: Early-Unregistration Sensitivity Disparity
- **Finding**: In the local repository test run ($N = 4,859$ enrollments), exactly 756 enrollments had an administrative unregistration date on or before Day 30. All 756 ($100.0\%$) resulted in the final outcome `Withdrawn`. Because these enrollments had largely or completely ceased interaction by Day 30, detecting them is relatively trivial.
- **Impact**: Excluding these 756 early-unregistered enrollments lowers ROC-AUC from $0.8487 \to 0.7931$ and At-Risk Recall from $76.12\% \to 67.24\%$ in that run.
- **Site Labeling**: Both `supplementary.json` and `SupplementarySection.tsx` display this plain statement without claims of "robust predictive ability", prominently labeled: `"Source run: local re-run (N=4,859), not comparable to the manuscript"`.

### Open Risk 2: Recomputed SHAP Attribution Divergence
- **Finding**: In `shap_analysis.py`, the reported manuscript shares ($33.22\%$ Academic, $32.61\%$ VLE, $34.17\%$ Static; combined dynamic $65.83\%$) are stored constants. When empirically recomputed using `shap.TreeExplainer` on the frozen model with 100 stratified background observations on the local test partition ($N = 4,859$ enrollments), the empirical shares diverge:
  - Academic Milestones: $32.72\%$ (vs. manuscript $33.22\%$, difference: $-0.50\text{ pp}$)
  - VLE Digital Engagement: $26.48\%$ (vs. manuscript $32.61\%$, difference: $-6.13\text{ pp}$)
  - Static Intake and Context: $40.80\%$ (vs. manuscript $34.17\%$, difference: $+6.63\text{ pp}$)
  - Combined Dynamic: $59.20\%$ (vs. manuscript $65.83\%$, difference: $-6.63\text{ pp}$)
  - Top feature in recomputation is `code_module` ($18.72\%$ share, mean $|\text{SHAP}| = 0.6607$), followed by `early_assessments_submitted_d30` ($16.99\%$) and `early_vle_incourse_clicks_d30` ($10.77\%$).
- **Site Labeling**: The differences are reported side-by-side in `SupplementarySection.tsx` without smoothing, labeled: `"Source run: local re-run (N=4,859), not comparable to the manuscript"`. Additionally, `ExplainabilitySection.tsx` links directly to `#supplementary` noting the different shares in the local run.

### Open Risk 3: Headline Numbers Not Regenerated by Current Repository
- **Finding**: The manuscript's headline numbers were not regenerated by the current repo. The local data artifacts reflect a partition of 22,859 / 4,875 / 4,859 enrollments, whereas the manuscript's locked evaluation was conducted on 22,815 / 4,889 / 4,889 enrollments ($20,149 / 4,318 / 4,318$ unique students).
- **Documentation**: Explicitly disclosed in `README.md`, `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, `oulad_student_prediction/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, and directly beneath the main heading of `SupplementarySection.tsx`: *"The manuscript's headline numbers were not regenerated by the current repository. The local artifacts reflect a 22,859 / 4,875 / 4,859 split, not the manuscript's 22,815 / 4,889 / 4,889."*
